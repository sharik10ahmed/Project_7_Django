WHO WE ARE

About FutureStack

At FutureStack we strive to nurture and shape aspirants' talents and personalities. We are committed to offer solutions to our esteemed clients after studying their area of business and analysing their needs. We deliver customized training and management development programs for corporates, government and private organisations, as well as for students of management, engineering and other professional institutes/establishments.

FutureStack offers consultancy in training, management development, project management, ISO 9001:2008, subject research and report, and guidance to MBA and engineering students for final year projects. With our panel of qualified professionals, who are also highly experienced domain experts, we shall endeavour to deliver solutions to completely satisfy and delight the client.

OUR APPROACH

Training Philosophy

At Erudition Infinite, we believe that true learning happens at the intersection of theory and practice. Our training methodology draws from military leadership principles, corporate best practices, and behavioral science research.

Experiential Learning

Learning by doing through simulations, case studies, and real-world scenarios.

Collaborative Growth

Peer learning and group activities that build teamwork and communication.

Measurable Outcomes

Every program has defined learning objectives with assessable outcomes.

OUR PURPOSE

Helping Others to Succeed

Erudition Infinite emphasizes bringing out the best in an individual or organization by helping them to identify their talents, set and accomplish goals, and succeed in their endeavors.

SWOT Analysis

Use of tools like SWOT analysis in conjunction with reflections on own life experiences, as aids to helping individuals find their mojo.

Self-Awareness & Goal Setting

Bringing about awareness of the significance of one's role at a personal level and in the organization's scheme of things is a critical deliverable in the Firm's T & D process.

Continuous Learning

The focus is on helping to navigate the thought process and then spurring oneself to align with one's desired personal or organizational goals.

Multiple Intelligences

The concept of multiple intelligences helps individuals develop attitudes that will help them acquire knowledge and skills, apply them correctly, and learn from experience.

Flagship Service: Personality Development

"Personality encompasses an infinite number of contributing factors. Individuals have to adapt to develop attitudes that will help them acquire knowledge and skills, apply them correctly, and learn from experience to permanently imbibe the most critical positive attributes."

In this program, we cover topics like integrity, self-motivation, goal setting, empathy, discipline, adaptability, effective communication, the pursuit of knowledge and excellence, presentation skills, etiquette, time and stress management, interpersonal relations, work-life balance, cross-cultural interaction, entrepreneurial skills, effective and ethical leadership, and many more.

— Lt Col Shreesh Kumar (Retd), Founder & CEO

Organizational Development Solutions

Besides development programs, Erudition Infinite also offers organizational development solutions, thinking ahead from individual to the corporate entity. Performing a needs analysis by looking at core business processes data, employees' expertise, perceived deficiencies, desired and actual outcomes, the Firm provides tailor-made re-organization solutions and customized programs to their clients.

Col Kumar's operational army combat experience and leadership stints in Corporate help steer the Firm's pursuit of excellence. Delivery of solutions in a non-contact manner via online platforms is also an option.
